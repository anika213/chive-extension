How to read this

Similar items adjacent: average-linkage hierarchy on cosine distance, with optimal leaf ordering within that tree. This is designed to expose blocks of related items.

Most central first: descending average cosine similarity to every other item in the same original cluster, excluding itself. A central item is broadly representative; a lower-ranked item can still belong to a coherent subgroup.

Subgroups: cut the within-cluster hierarchy into 1–12 groups. These are exploratory numeric partitions, not semantic labels or an automatically selected optimum. All original HDBSCAN assignments remain unchanged.

All similarities use the original normalized MPNet behaviour vectors, not the 10-dimensional UMAP coordinates. Sources, facets, and runs are not combined.
